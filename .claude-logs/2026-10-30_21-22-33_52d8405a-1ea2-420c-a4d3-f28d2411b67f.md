**Pronunciation:** PRO-tuh-zhay, with the stress on the first syllable. The "zh" is the soft sound in "measure", and the end rhymes with "day". In phonetic spelling, that's /ˈproʊtəˌʒeɪ/ in American English; British speakers often say /ˈprɒtɪʒeɪ/, with a short "o" as in "pot".

**Origin:** it's French. *Protégé* is the past participle of *protéger*, "to protect", so it literally means "protected one". The French verb comes from Latin *protegere*, from *pro-* ("in front") and *tegere* ("to cover"). English took it up in the late 1700s for someone guided and supported by an older, more experienced person.

**The protégé effect** is the name for the finding that people learn material better when they teach it, or prepare to teach it. That's why the name fits your app, though there's a twist: the user is the mentor and the AI students are the protégés, yet the user is the one who learns.

The drawback as a product name is that many people won't know how to say it, and the accent is awkward in a URL, so you'd write "protege" there.
