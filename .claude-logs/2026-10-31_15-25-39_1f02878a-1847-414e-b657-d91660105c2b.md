Scoring is the step right after the class ends, but it isn't built yet. In today's app, the class stops at "You answered 2 questions." This is the plan from [brief.md](docs/product/brief.md), and it's Arif's part (8X-15).

**What happens next**
1. The class ends after 4 questions or when you press End class.
2. The AI scores your explanation, and you get an Assessment screen.
3. Every concept you missed or got wrong is saved as a gap.
4. Each gap comes back later as a one-minute warm-up from a student who remembers you.

**What it's based on**

Your explanation is compared with the key concepts found in that video, like the 7 we saw for the Steve Jobs speech. The AI gets your explanation, the key concepts and the transcript, then marks each concept:

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Correct** | You explained it properly |
| **Missed** | You left it out |
| **Wrong** | You said something incorrect |

That gives the headline score, like "6 of 8". The brief doesn't define a pass mark, and the score isn't a grade on how well you write.

Questions are tracked separately, as answered, needed help or skipped. The app already saves these. Students also add a short in-character reaction, but the brief doesn't say this changes the score.

The score stays hidden until the class ends, so you explain from memory instead of chasing points.

**Why it matters**

The score isn't the goal; it's how the app finds out what didn't stick. Without it, nothing connects a class to the next one:
- **It shows your gaps:** you see exactly what you missed, with a timestamp to rewatch that moment in the video.
- **It drives the warm-ups:** a gap is due after 2 days. If you get it right, it's due again 5 days later, and a second right answer closes it. If you get it wrong, it's due again in 2 days.
- **It delivers the theme:** those reviews around day 2–3 and day 7 are what make this "Learning that sticks".

So without scoring, there are no gaps, warm-ups or topic page. That's why it's the main blocker, and Arif should say today whether it's on track, since the brief wants the whole loop ready for Feedback 2 on Monday.
