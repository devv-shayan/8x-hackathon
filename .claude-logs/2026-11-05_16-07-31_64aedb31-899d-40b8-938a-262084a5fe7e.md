**Chalkback in one line:** you learn from a YouTube video by teaching it to AI students (Maya and Omar), and whatever you got wrong comes back to you days later so it sticks.

- Every key concept you **missed or explained wrong** in step 2 becomes a gap: one row in the database tied to that concept and its video.
- It's based on your *first* explanation, so even a concept you fixed during the class still comes back later.
- Each gap has a due date. That due date is what Maya's memory shows: the idea fading day by day until it's due.

**Warm-ups** are how a gap comes back to you.
- When a gap is due, a student from that class asks you about it again: one question, about a minute.
- Warm-ups show up on Home, while a new video is being read (to fill the wait), or from "Redraw it with Maya" on her memory board.

| What happens                 | Next due        |
| ---------------------------- | --------------- |
| Gap found in a class         | 2 days later    |
| Warm-up answered **right**   | 5 days later    |
| Right a **second** time      | Closed: Maya keeps it ("Kept for good") |
| Warm-up answered **wrong**   | 2 days later again |
