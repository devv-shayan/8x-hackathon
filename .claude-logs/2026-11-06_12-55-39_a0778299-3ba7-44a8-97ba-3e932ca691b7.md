Here's the updated info for both videos. It comes from the current code and `origin/main`, because [demo-script.md](docs/product/demo-script.md) is out of date. I haven't re-tested these flows on the live site for this write-up.

## Shared context
- **Chalkback:** you paste a YouTube video, explain it in your own words, then teach it to two AI students, Maya and Omar. They ask about what you missed.
- **Main difference from other apps:** the students remember you and come back to what you missed.

## Video 1: Class memory and warm-ups

**Class memory: what your class might forget**
- **What it is:** a board of the ideas your explanations missed, each tied to its video and the student who will ask about it.
- **Where to open it:** the "Your class's memory" row on Home.
- **Idea due today:** the student raises a hand and says, "I'm forgetting 'X'. Can you explain it to me again?" Below is an **Answer Maya's question** button.
- **Nothing due:** the student says, "No questions today! I'll ask about 'X' in 2 days." Warm-ups can't be answered early.
- **Rest of the board:** **Coming up** lists upcoming ideas with who asks and when. **Learned for good** holds ideas you got right twice in a row.

**Warm-ups: the student comes back**
- **How one starts:** every idea missed or wrong in your first explanation becomes a gap, even if you fixed it later in class.
- **Schedule:**

| Event | What happens next |
|---|---|
| Gap found | Due 2 days later |
| Right answer | Due again 5 days later |
| Second right in a row | Closed |
| Wrong answer | Due again in 2 days, and the streak resets |

- **Where it appears:** a "Maya has a question" card on Home, the Class memory board, or while a new video is being read, so it fills the wait.
- **The question:** the student who spoke with you on that video is picked when there is one. They ask a new question aloud and never give the answer away.
- **Answering:** hold to speak or type, or tap **I don't remember**. The screen also shows the missed idea and its timestamp, such as "You missed this one at 2:14".
- **Judging:** AI checks the idea against the video, so your own words count, but vague answers are marked wrong.
- **Result lines:**
    - "That's right. It comes back Saturday."
    - "Not quite. It comes back Monday."
    - "Two right in a row. This one is closed."
- **History:** the video's topic page shows each gap and how its past warm-ups went.

**Suggested flow:** Home → Class memory board → Answer Maya's question → speak an answer → result → topic page.

## Video 2: Daily quiz
- **What it is:** a once-a-day game of 3–5 questions on ideas from videos you've already taught, played to beat your own best score. It needs at least 3 ideas and resets at your midnight.
- **Entry:** a black **Daily quiz** card on Home: "A minute on what you've taught. Set a score to beat." Later it reads "Your best: 3 of 5. Can you beat it?" and disappears once you've played that day.
- **Question types:** fill in the blank, multiple choice, and true or false.
- **Background:** shimmering chalk words from your own ideas, which flash lime on a right answer.
- **After each answer:**
    - A stamp lands, with chalk dust if you're right.
    - The card flips to the real line, such as "The video says, 2:14".
    - Maya and Omar react, for example "You and Omar got it. Maya fell for it."
    - The You, Maya and Omar scores update, and "On a roll!" appears after two right in a row.
- **End screen:** your score counts up with a headline: "Your first score", "Your best yet", "Matched your best" or "So close". A line such as "You beat Maya and tied Omar" follows.
- **Reminder:** **Remind me about tomorrow's quiz** opens Google Calendar, pre-filled with a daily reminder for 7 days.
- **Why it's different from warm-ups:** warm-ups have you explain what you missed, on a schedule. The quiz is a quick tap-to-answer game across everything you've taught.

**Suggested flow:** Home card → one question of each type, with one wrong on purpose → end screen → calendar reminder → Home with the card gone.

## Don't claim these on camera
- **"The app reminds you":** Class memory push reminders need a store build, so they don't work in a browser or Expo Go. Only the quiz's calendar reminder works today.
- **"Maya and Omar really answer the quiz":** their results are simulated, with Omar right about 85% of the time and Maya about 45%.
- **"Quiz questions can never be wrong":** code sets the answer key and cuts fill-in-the-blank answers from the video's line, but AI still writes the wording.
- **"Wrong quiz answers come back as warm-ups" or "your score follows you":** the score is saved only on that device or browser, and quiz answers don't create gaps.
- **"Maya's memory":** this is the old name.
